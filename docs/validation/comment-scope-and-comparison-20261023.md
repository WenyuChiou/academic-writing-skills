# Comment scope and comparison-layer acceptance — 2026-10-23

## Scope and diagnosis

This increment retains the released writing workflow and existing operation
signatures. Existing rules already required comparison purpose, coherent
evidence/meaning units and a substantive answer without an RQ label. The observed
writing failure was chiefly execution and acceptance, not absence of that principle.
Sentence-pair continuity had been treated as sufficient even when the contribution
of the complete analytical layer was missing.

The narrow clarification asks the existing reader walkthrough to recover what
aspect the layer compares, what its evidence establishes, and what the next
comparison adds or must account for. It introduces neither a new checklist nor a
mandatory sentence template, analysis sequence or diagnostic pass/fail gate.

## Fixed behavior controls and actual proposals

Criteria were fixed before generation. Separate fresh-context AI writers used
released 1.3.21 and the 1.3.22 candidate on the same ten synthetic requests from
`evals/comparison_layer_cases.json`. They supplied proposed prose, plans, scope
records, reader paraphrases and uncertainty statements. Neither writer received
the expected repair, evaluation criteria or the other condition's output. The
parent read every resulting passage against the fixed facts and criteria; writer
paraphrases were not accepted as proof of comprehension.

Thus the bounded outcome is ten locally satisfactory behaviors in each condition,
not demonstrated comparative improvement. A stray transcription marker in the
frozen synthetic input was corrected by both writers; its later removal from the
public fixture changes no fact or criterion. A separate local application used
author-supplied material read-only and was not redistributed. Both conditions
preserved its facts and advisor wording and made the measurement-input implication
explicit; no manuscript or supplement was edited. Source, table and layout limits
remain separate from the prose-proposal verdict.

Sixteen additional synthetic comment-scope requests were inspected from actual
minimal OOXML fixtures: twelve proposed repairs, one no-change and three correctly
withheld ambiguous/deleted/multiple targets. One locked adjacent dependency remains
open by design. Parent inspection confirmed current facts, declared diffs, preserved
unselected wording, explicit scope expansion, reply treatment and grammar-only
restraint. These are proposals, not completed Word edits or saved-layout evidence.

This is one bounded paired AI-writing exercise plus a separate scope-proposal
batch. It is not human-reader validation, a blinded reader experiment, statistical
recomputation, real-document editing/layout certification or proof of general
quality, efficiency or token savings. No manuscript analyses or simulations were
rerun; the writing exercises used native agents. The retained interpretation is
an execution fix with more explicit acceptance
evidence, not a claim that adding rules automatically improves writing.
